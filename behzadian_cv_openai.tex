\section{Profile}
Research scientist with a Ph.D. in reinforcement learning studying policy improvement when the model or evaluator is imperfect. My work spans robust MDPs under model uncertainty, recurrent value evaluators with verifier-style feedback, and production experimentation systems for measurement correctness and reliability. Current interests include RL post-training, reasoning, long-horizon agents, and reliable tool-use decisions under uncertainty.

\section{Selected Research}

\cvitem{}{%
\textbf{Policy Improvement with Imperfect Evaluators}\newline
\emph{Finite-Reference Error Bounds for Recurrent Value Evaluators in Policy Improvement (UPI-TRM); earlier version at ICLR 2026 Workshop on AI with Recursive Self-Improvement}\newline
Co-developed finite-depth error bounds for recurrent value evaluators used in policy improvement without assuming recurrent convergence, separating endpoint discrepancy from Bellman-residual error. Connected centered advantage error to conservative policy-improvement bounds and evaluated the analysis on an exact finite MDP and a fixed Sudoku census.
}

\cvitem{}{%
\textbf{Robust and Risk-Sensitive Markov Decision Processes}\newline
\emph{NeurIPS 2021 and AISTATS 2021}\newline
Developed scalable algorithms for \(L_\infty\)-constrained S-rectangular robust MDPs and percentile criteria under model uncertainty.
}
