\section{Selected Technical Work}

\cvitem{}{%
\textbf{Policy Improvement under Imperfect Evaluators (UPI-TRM)}\newline
\emph{ICLR 2026 Workshop on AI with Recursive Self-Improvement}\newline
Co-led research on policy improvement with truncated recurrent evaluators and verifier feedback. Derived bounds separating Bellman-residual error from truncation bias and linked evaluator error to conservative policy-improvement guarantees. On hard 4$\times$4 Sudoku, persistent-latent UPI-TRM achieved 57.4\% $\pm$ 12.2\% over 10 seeds versus 32.0\% $\pm$ 15.3\% for TRM+PPO.
}

\cvitem{}{%
\textbf{Research-Scale Language-Model Post-Training}\newline
Built PPO and DPO experiments on small language models for exact-answer arithmetic tasks: generated synthetic prompts and targets, used verifier-style feedback, and evaluated outputs by exact-match accuracy. Research-scale implementation on small models.
}
